01 · The Beginning

It all began around 6:30 pm on 28th March, 2005. The very day I was born, my family had mixed feelings but somehow my mother knew I would the child she ever dreamed of!

"Every story begins before we know it has started. Mine begins with small moments, familiar voices, music in the background, stories that made me curious, and the feeling that there was always something new to discover.

I am still learning what shaped me most. Maybe it was the people who believed in me, the experiences that challenged me, the days I was left alone to take care of family, e=when parents were gone or the ordinary days that taught me to notice the little things. They all belong here.

This chapter reminds me that I do not have to come from a perfect beginning to create a meaningful story. I only have to keep moving forward with an open mind.

02 · The Learner

Today, I am a final year computer science student. And guess what after all the hardships, I finally grabed an internship. I spend my time learning how to break a problem into smaller pieces, think logically, and turn an idea into something that works. Sometimes the answer comes quickly. Sometimes I need to start again, and that is part of learning which I learned the hard way.

For me, code is more than syntax. It is patience, curiosity, teamwork, and the satisfaction of solving something that once felt impossible. Every project and every mistake adds a new line to my story.

I am not expected to know everything yet. I am here to ask better questions, practise, experiment, and become a little more capable than I was yesterday.

03 · The Dreamer

I dream of building a future where I can use what I learn to solve real problems, create things that matter, and keep discovering what I am capable of. I want to grow into someone who brings both skill and kindness into the rooms I enter.

My dreams are not only about a job or a destination. They include experiences, meaningful friendships, new places, songs I sing without overthinking, books I get lost in, and moments worth capturing. That childhood curiosity is still alive in me, and I want to keep it that way. The dream of becoming a singer is still alive in me. I want to keep it that way.

I may not know exactly how every dream will happen. For now, I will give them effort, give myself time, and stay open to opportunities I cannot see yet.

04 · The Becoming

I am learning that becoming is not a straight line. There will be plans that change, days that test my patience, and moments when other people seem to be moving faster and trust me the moment you see your friends getting placed before you and the sudden change in their behavior, that breaks you differently. I want to remember that growth is not a race.

I want to become confident without losing my humility, ambitious without forgetting to rest, independent without being afraid to ask for help, and successful without losing sight of what makes life feel like mine. Most importantly, not giving up on my roots instilled in me by my parents.

This chapter has no final paragraph. I will keep adding to it through the choices I make, the people I care for, and the courage to begin again. The next page is still mine to write. We have a long way to go, one step at a time.

“I don't need to have the whole story figured out. I just need the courage to turn the next page.”

The final chapter · A letter across time

Dear Future Me.

Hey you,

I wonder where you are as you read this. I hope you have found a little more courage, a little more peace, and a few reasons to be proud of yourself.

Right now, I am still figuring things out. I am in the final year of college as acomputer science student learning to turn ideas into something real, one problem, one project, and one small breakthrough at a time. Some days I feel unstoppable. Other days, even the simplest thing takes extra effort. I hope you remember both kinds of days, because they are both part of our story. Its a bit overwhelming, the last year. Losing friends, going more far away from family. I hope it all worked out.

I hope you kept your curiosity. I hope you still get excited when a line of code finally works, when a song feels like it understands you, when a good book makes the world feel bigger and you get lost in it, or when a photograph captures a moment you wish could stay forever.

I hope you are building a career you are proud of—but I hope you are building a life you love, too. Make room for music. Make room for people who make you feel safe to be yourself, and who make you feel loved. Make room for rest. You do not have to earn every moment of happiness. Prioritize yourself, Hopefully you have learnt to let go people who don't value you enough.

If things did not go exactly as planned, I hope you are being gentle with us. A changed direction is not a failed story. It is still ours, and we can write the next page from wherever we are.

So, future me, keep learning. Keep creating. Keep showing up. Be ambitious, but do not become a stranger to yourself while chasing your dreams.

With hope, a little fear, and a lot of belief,
Your past self ♡
